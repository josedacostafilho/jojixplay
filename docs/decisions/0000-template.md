## Context

Describe the concrete problem, confirmed constraints, and forces that make a durable decision necessary. Distinguish facts from assumptions.

## Decision

State the selected approach as an enforceable rule. Define its scope and boundaries.

## Consequences

### Benefits

- List the outcomes this choice enables.

### Costs and risks

- List real tradeoffs, operational implications, and failure modes.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative name

Explain why it was not selected. Do not invent alternatives merely to make the decision look thorough.
