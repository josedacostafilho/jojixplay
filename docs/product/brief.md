# Product brief

JojixPlay is a free, private, movement-controlled game collection running entirely on a landscape phone. Players prop up the phone and use their bodies to control Draw, Bubbles, and Racing. Operating-system or wired screen mirroring makes the phone's screen visible on a television; the television runs no app.

The differentiator is the games. Camera frames and pose landmarks remain on-device, with no accounts, backend, persistence, signaling, or peer connection. One or two players use ephemeral playfield-local state. See [Phone play](local-play.md), [Camera orientation](camera-orientation.md), and the [game contracts](../README.md).

Real-device usability, performance, and external-mirroring acceptance are required before release.
