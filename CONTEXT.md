# JojixPlay language

## Corrida dos Blocos

**Run**: One attempt from the held-crouch start to victory, loss of all hearts, or exit. Its five-minute duration counts active running only.

**Level**: One of three consecutive portions of a run. Entering a new level restores three hearts and adds an obstacle type.

**Arrival**: The instant an approaching obstacle reaches the player. Jump and duck actions have generous timing windows around arrival; pose walls require a matching pose at arrival.

**Pose wall**: An approaching wall with a body-shaped opening and a live preview of the player's pose. Its target describes arm positions, not exact body proportions.

**Heart**: One remaining chance to miss an obstacle. A miss removes one heart; losing the third ends the run.
